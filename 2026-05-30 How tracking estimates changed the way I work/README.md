# How tracking estimates changed the way I work

This presentation by Thomas Aglassinger at the [Barcamp Graz 2026](https://barcamp-graz.org/) explores how moving from project-level time tracking to task-level tracking and monitoring the "blow-up factor" can significantly improve work processes and estimation accuracy.

## Summary

Shifting from tracking time per project (useful for billing) to tracking time per individual task/ticket to gain actionable insights. In combination with estimates on a task-level, the blow-up factor (defined as actual time divided by estimated time) can be monitored.

Some strategies for improving the blow-up factor are:

- Estimate almost everything and check the blow-up factor.
- Split tasks in manageable sizes.
- Re-estimate if you realize early that the estimate is off.
- For tasks with a high blow-up factor, find similarities.

Continuously applying this can significantly improve the accuracy of estimates.
